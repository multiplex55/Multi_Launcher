# Coverage matrix — achieved depth and planned traces

Checkpoints: G01/M1-A through M3-A, 2026-10-28. Source: `59156d96adcc73939c7171487a0cb582e3286587`. This matrix covers all 105 buckets in [SOURCE_INVENTORY.md](evidence/SOURCE_INVENTORY.md), with supporting non-bucket boundaries below. Main rows show current achieved depth; explicitly labeled earlier snapshots retain history. It is a risk-weighted inspection ledger, not a claim that 790 files received deep review.

Depth means **achieved at this checkpoint**:

- `DEEP_TRACE`: a bounded named flow was read through its producer/owner/consumer and rejection/shutdown branches. This never means the entire bucket was audited.
- `TARGETED_REVIEW`: selected implementation bodies/types and adjacent wiring were inspected; the stated boundary is the limit.
- `SURVEY_ONLY`: tracked presence, module/facade declarations, signatures or manifest exposure only; no behavior conclusion.
- `OUT_OF_SCOPE`: implementation intentionally excluded with a reason.
- `UNAVAILABLE`: required evidence could not be obtained. No present first-party source bucket needs this disposition at M1-A; runtime/native evidence is unavailable by task design, not missing source.

The planned column is a **future packet**, not completed work. Group names below identify navigation/risk families and do not introduce new architecture.
